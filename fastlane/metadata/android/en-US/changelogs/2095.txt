New "Touch calibration" setting under Settings → Typing. Choose Conservative, Normal, or Rescue-heavy to control how aggressively taps that land between keys (gap rescue) or on a neighbouring key (adaptive spatial model) are re-attributed. Conservative prefers exactly the key you touched; Rescue-heavy widens dead zones and corrects neighbour misses for heavier thumbs. Normal reproduces the previous behaviour, so existing typing feel is unchanged unless you switch profiles.
